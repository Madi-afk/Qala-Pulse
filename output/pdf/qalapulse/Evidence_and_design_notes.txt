DESIGN REVIEW
Two A4 compositions were created: a light editorial version and a navy hero version.
Both were rendered to PNG and visually compared. The light version was selected:
it preserves more whitespace, gives the phone a clear silhouette and uses less ink.
The custom pulse mark and schematic map are original vector artwork. The phone is
an English concept visualization based on existing product behavior; it is not a
screenshot, an English localization claim, or evidence of live incident activity.
Its blue palette follows the investor brief; the implemented app uses a different theme.

VALIDATION
The PDF was checked for one page, A4 dimensions, required copy and extractable text.
No raster images are embedded in the PDF. Inter Regular/SemiBold/Bold are embedded.
All text bounds are checked by the generator. Final PDF rendering was visually
inspected for clipping, overlap, alignment and hierarchy.

EDITING
Open QalaPulse_OnePager.html in a browser. Click a text line and use the editing panel to change text, size, weight, color and position. Undo/redo and browser-local autosave are supported. Download HTML saves a portable editable copy. Lines retain fixed positions and do not reflow automatically; inspect the layout before export.
Use Print / Save as PDF with A4 paper, no margins and background graphics enabled.
For reproducible PDF/HTML generation, edit scripts/build_qalapulse_onepager.py and
run it with Python containing reportlab and pypdf. Use Poppler pdftoppm at 300 dpi
to regenerate QalaPulse_Preview.png. Font files and the OFL license are in assets/.
The PDF, PNG and HTML share the same drawing geometry.
